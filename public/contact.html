Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can responders access my information?

With SafePass ID, responders can scan your QR code and instantly access your critical medical information. No waiting times - immediate access when every second counts.

Is my information secure?

Yes, all your information is encrypted and stored securely. You control exactly what information is shared and with whom through our privacy settings.

How do I share my QR code?

You can display your QR code on jewelry, store it on your phone, or print it. When responders scan it, they immediately see the information you've marked as publicly visible.

Can I update my information?

Absolutely! You can update your profile anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately.

What if I report my profile as lost/stolen?

You can quickly disable your QR code from your dashboard. Once disabled, the QR code will no longer grant access to your profile.
